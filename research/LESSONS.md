# Lessons and historical benchmarks
Historical reported totals per 50 decisions: original 1,524,032; first compact 1,518,580; E012 integration 212,057; fresh rebuild 176,783 (including consolidation). Fresh rebuild run https://github.com/magickmomo/nimo/actions/runs/45964624365, artifact 15182048868: 50 decisions, 8 consolidation calls, 19/20 travel successes, 28 episodes, 3 semantic memories, no reflective memories. Blue fruit repeatedly eaten despite no fullness gain; red fruit reduced fullness; amber not discovered. Recall of all three memories does not prove scalable relevance. Fresh and mature states differ; no causal superiority claim. The [mature snapshot audit](experiments/W003-E00/results.md) verified 253,320 tokens over 50 decisions and 12 completed journeys in 16 attempts. Stored fullness of 65.16% became 0% at the first decision, which limits comparisons with the fresh run.
Lessons: compact context reduces overhead; verified outcomes and evidence-backed memory are necessary but not sufficient for application; avoid domain-specific cognitive fixes; navigation requires reachable coordinates and precise failures; preserve time semantics in snapshots.
